## Do tonight

### 1. "Yes, and" — amend the scene that is on air
**Pitch.** A second input next to the caption bar: "change this scene", not "queue a new one". The
shot keeps running and one thing about it changes.
**Fun.** O3 says their best users already try to do this and the site fakes it. Here the cat you are
looking at grows a hat while you look at it. It also rewards watching over queueing.
**Criterion.** fal: *"A clear creative concept, and a use of Director that a simple text-to-video call
couldn't achieve."* Nothing states it harder — an amend has no meaning without a running session.
**Mechanism.** `channel.ts`: `Idea` gets `kind: "new" | "amend"`; amends skip the one-per-user queue
rule but still respect `STEER_GAP_MS`. `showrunner.ts`: a third agent (or a mode branch in
`writeSteer`) whose instructions are "restate the subject exactly, change only what the viewer asks,
do not move the camera". `index.ts` `/say` takes `kind`. Broadcaster is unchanged — the same
`sendSteer()` → `{type:"prompt", prompt, prompt_version}` in `broadcaster.html:585`.
`index.html`: an "amend" affordance on `#caption-bar`. `telegram.ts`: `submitIdea` gains `amend`.
**Effort** 2 h. **Risk** low-medium: the model may over-rewrite. Mitigate in the instructions (name
the subject, change one attribute) and keep the amend prompt short.

### 2. Hold-to-talk: say the idea out loud (SLNG STT)
**Pitch.** Hold a button on the viewer page, speak, the words become your idea. Already scoped in
PLAN §5 and never built — today SLNG is TTS only.
**Fun.** Speaking a wish at a TV is a different act from typing it, and it is the one interaction that
reads instantly on a phone in a demo room.
**Criterion.** SLNG: *"Use SLNG at the core of your project, whether that's the speech-to-text API,
the text-to-speech API, or a full voice agent on top."* Two surfaces beats one. Also
*"Real numbers where you have them, like latency, cost"* — mirror `announcer.ts`'s `slng_tts ms=` log
line as `slng_stt ms=` and we have both halves of the latency table for free.
**Mechanism.** `index.html`: `MediaRecorder` → webm/opus blob → `POST /say-voice`. New route in
`index.ts` posts the blob unchanged to `https://eu-west.api.slng.ai/v1/stt/<model>` (`docs/cards/slng.md`
confirms browser webm/opus is accepted as-is), then falls straight into the existing `/say` path so
moderation still runs first. `announcer.ts` is the template for the fetch and the timing log.
**Effort** 1.5 h. **Risk** low; the STT model id needs one look at the SLNG dashboard.

### 3. Vonage live captions on the announcer
**Pitch.** A `CC` toggle. The spoken "Up next, from Timba…" appears as real broadcast subtitles.
**Fun.** Small, but it is the detail that makes it read as a TV channel rather than a web page.
**Criterion.** Vonage: *"Use of Vonage Video API: how effectively and deeply is the API integrated?"*
and *"User Experience: is the interface intuitive and well-designed?"* — two of the five scored areas,
and it is the cheapest way off "we used it as a pipe".
**Mechanism.** We already mix the SLNG clip into the published track (`setupAudioGraph()` /
`playAnnouncer()` in `broadcaster.html:272`), which is exactly the condition `docs/cards/vonage.md` §6
flags: captions transcribe *session* audio, so a separate `<audio>` would be invisible. Add
`publishCaptions: true` to the `OT.initPublisher` options in `publishToVonage()`; viewers call
`subscribeToCaptions(true)` and render `captionReceived` (`text`, `isFinal`) into a subtitle strip in
`index.html`. Gate display on the announcer window (we know it: `duckDirectorGain()` is already
called around the clip) so Director's ambience does not produce nonsense captions.
**Effort** 1 h. **Risk** low. Routed session and captions-on-by-default are both verified.

### 4. Format modes — `script` beats turn the subject into a TV format
**Pitch.** Chips: `NEWS` · `NATURE DOC` · `GAME SHOW` · `AD`. Pick one and whatever is on screen is
re-staged into that format without a cut — the cats become the anchors.
**Fun.** O5: this is what people ask for and cannot get. And it is legible in one second on screen.
**Criterion.** fal, same quote as #1, from the other direction: `script` is timed structure inside one
running shot. A t2v call has no timeline to write beats onto.
**Mechanism.** `broadcaster.html` `sendSteer()` gains an optional `script` array and
`script_mode: "replace"` on the `{type:"prompt"}` send (`docs/cards/director.md` §2;
`script_max_beats: 64`). `showrunner.ts` returns `{prompt, script}` via `structuredOutput` — a
3-beat template per format (0 s establish, ~6 s cut-in, ~12 s sign-off). `index.html` chips post
`format` to `/say`. Watch the §8 gotcha: `script` is incompatible with `end_image_url`/`audio_url` at
configure time, so only send it on `prompt` messages.
**Effort** 2 h. **Risk** medium — beat timing is unverified on our key; falls back to a plain prompt.

### 5. Cast & props strip — make the continuity visible
**Pitch.** A rail strip: `ON SET: capybara · rubber duck (7 scenes)`. Anyone can write "bring back the
duck" and the sceneWriter reinserts it from the stored description.
**Fun.** This is the running-joke mechanic. O6 shows theirs is a prompt trick; ours is the model's own
memory, and a counter that keeps climbing is what makes people try to keep something alive.
**Criterion.** Vonage: *"Creativity: is the idea original, imaginative, and fun?"* Also the strongest
single piece of on-screen evidence for the fal claim — the prop survives steers nobody re-described.
**Mechanism.** `showrunner.ts`: `writeSteer` returns `{prompt, cast: string[]}` via `structuredOutput`.
`channel.ts`: `Scene` gains `cast`, plus a `props` map of name → first-seen scene count; expose it in
`Status`. `index.html`: render into `#rail` above `#rail-queue`. `telegram.ts` `whatsOn` reports it too.
**Effort** 1.5 h. **Risk** low. Costs a few output tokens per steer; `spend.ts` already meters that.

### 6. Telegram standby slot — the bot gives you airtime while you are away
**Pitch.** `/standby <premise>`. The bot remembers it. When the queue empties, it DMs
"the channel is idle — air your bit now?" with Approve / Deny. Approve puts it on air.
**Fun.** You get airtime you would otherwise have missed, and the channel never shows dead air.
**Criterion.** Mastra: *"Someone would keep it (20 points): a clear user and a clear problem. Would the
judge leave the bot in their contacts?"* — a bot that hands you a slot on a live channel is worth
keeping; one that relays messages is not. It also hits *"Act: it calls tools that change something in
the world, and asks for approval in the chat before anything risky (requireApproval renders Approve /
Deny cards)"* and the "message first" bullet, so `More than a wrapper (30 points)` stops resting on
memory alone.
**Mechanism.** `telegram.ts`: new `setStandby` tool storing the premise on the existing per-user
`Memory` (`lastMessages: 10` today), plus a `requireApproval` tool for the airing. `sendDM()` at
`telegram.ts:290` is the DM path and already works. `index.ts` `/b/:secret/next-steer` asks the
standby pool when `channel.nextIdea()` is undefined, instead of returning 204 into the ident card.
**Effort** 2.5 h. **Risk** medium: `requireApproval` rendering on the Telegram channel is unverified —
timebox 30 min, then fall back to a plain "reply YES in 60 s" and keep the rest.

## Later

### 7. Sponsor ad break, generated live
"And now, a word from our sponsors" — the channel morphs into a 15 s ad for one of the event's
sponsors, with a gold `AD` caption and a spoken read. O2 shows the shape works. Shares the `script`
plumbing from #4 and the SLNG read path the karma-voice work is already building, so it is ~1.5 h on
top of both. Demoing an ad for fal, generated live by fal, in front of fal, is the single best 15
seconds available to us. Keep it logo-free and text-free — the moderator prompt in `showrunner.ts`
already forbids brands and on-screen text, so the ad has to be written around that, not through it.

### 8. Vonage signalling replaces the 1 s `/status` poll
Push chat, queue and likes over the Vonage data plane (`session.signal`) so a heart bursts on every
screen at the same instant. Scores hard on Vonage depth. Blocker to check first: viewers hold
`subscriber` tokens, and subscriber role almost certainly cannot *send* signals — so likes would stay
on `POST /like` and only the server→viewers direction moves to signalling, via the REST signal API
with the application JWT (`vonage.ts`). Unverified in `docs/cards/vonage.md`; 20 min to settle.

### 9. Archive reruns instead of the ident card
Vonage archiving needs no account toggle and produces an MP4. When Director stops after 120 s idle,
publish last night's archive instead of `drawIdentFrame()`. Kills dead air and cuts fal spend — and
the spend meter makes that visible. Archive method names in `@vonage/video@1.31.1` unverified.

### 10. Karma derby
When the top two by karma both have an idea queued, air them back to back with a VS bar; likes in each
window decide, and the winner's next idea skips the queue. Pure rivalry, reuses `like()` and
`orderedQueue()` in `channel.ts`. Needs ~50 s of screen time, so it does not fit a 60 s demo.

### 11. RTMP out to Twitch/YouTube
One Vonage broadcast call and the channel is on a real TV platform judges can open on their own
phones. ~5 s extra latency, fine for a second-screen. Mostly a narrative win for "Impact & Potential".

### 12. Photo → `end_image_url`
Send the bot a photo; the shot morphs until it *arrives* at your photo, then keeps going. Genuinely
Director-only. Held back for one reason: we moderate text, not images, and `POST /say` fails closed by
design. An unmoderated image path onto a public stream is the one thing that can end the demo badly.
Only with a vision moderation pass, or restricted to a known handful of users.

## Do not copy

- **Rerun and tag channels (O8).** 24 tags weighted into the tens of thousands. We will have ~30
  scenes by demo time. A channel switcher over 30 clips is an empty menu.
- **Profiles and the follow graph.** Needs a population; we have a room.
- **`buy ad` and the $8,500 prize pool.** They have sponsor money and a payments surface. Building a
  fake one is scope with no payoff, and it is the most obviously copied part of the product.
- **Their visual signature.** The Shrikhand wordmark with the stacked black extrusion, the four splash
  lines, "tap the screen to ♥ like". The fal judges sponsor the original and will recognise every one
  of them. Our own type and our own copy; PLAN §0 already rules out the name.
- **Queue upvoting.** With twenty people in a room it is either unanimous or noise. Karma order plus
  one idea per person is the better fit and is already built.
- **The independent-clip pipeline itself** — 4 workers, per-clip HLS discontinuities, continuity faked
  in the prompt. Borrowing any of it would undercut the only claim we are making to fal.
- **Anything needing moderation we cannot staff:** image or audio upload, freeform on-screen text,
  Turnstile-scale anonymous traffic. Fail-closed text moderation sized to a room is correct here.
